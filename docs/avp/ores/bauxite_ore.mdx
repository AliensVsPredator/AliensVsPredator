### Features/Generation

Bauxite Ore generates medium to large sizes in the Overworld. Default values are as follows:

#### Bauxite Ore Middle
- **Feature ID**: `avp:bauxite_ore_middle`
- **Count**: 20
- **Height Range**: Uniform (9 to 79)

#### Bauxite Ore Upper
- **Feature ID**: `avp:bauxite_ore_upper`
- **Count**: 75
- **Height Range**: Trapezoid (0 to 512)
