### Features/Generation

Zinc Ore generates in the Overworld. Default values are as follows:

#### Normal
- **Feature ID**: `avp:zinc_ore`
- **Count**: 16
- **Height Range**: Trapezoid (-16 to 112)

#### Dripstone Caves
- **Feature ID**: `avp:zinc_ore_dripstone_caves`
- **Count**: 16
- **Height Range**: Trapezoid (-16 to 112)
