### Features/Generation

Monazite Ore generates in the Overworld, with larger ore groups in jungles. Default values are as follows:

#### Normal
- **Feature ID**: `avp:monazite_ore`
- **Count**: 2
- **Height Range**: Uniform (0 to 16)

#### Jungle
- **Feature ID**: `avp:monazite_ore_jungle`
- **Count**: 15
- **Height Range**: Trapezoid (-48 to 48)
