**Be mindful of mining Lithium Ore blocks near water sources, as their explosive nature could cause unintended destruction.**
</Callout>

## Description

Lithium Ore can be mined to obtain [Lithium Dust](../items/lithium_dust). This reactive ore is key to items that deal with electronics and power.

### Behavior

Lithium Ore has a unique interaction with water. If water comes in contact with Lithium Ore, the block explodes with a strength of 1/2 tnt.

### Generation
Lithium ore can be found anywhere in the world between Y=9-40 however, it is found at much higher concentration in deserts between Y=9-64.
