## Description

**Lithium Ore** can be mined and processed to obtain lithium. This ore is reactive in certain situations and serves as a key resource for advanced crafting within the game.

### Behavior

Lithium Ore has a unique interaction with water. If water comes in contact with Lithium Ore, the block explodes with a strength of `2F`.

### Features/Generation

Lithium Ore generates in the Overworld, with larger ore groups in deserts. Default values are as follows:

#### Normal
- **Feature ID**: `avp:lithium_ore`
- **Count**: 8
- **Generation Height Range**: Uniform (9 to 40)

#### Desert
- **Feature ID**: `avp:lithium_ore_desert`
- **Count**: 20
- **Generation Height Range**: Uniform (9 to 64)
