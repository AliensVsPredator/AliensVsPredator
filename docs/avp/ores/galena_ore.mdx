## Description
Galena is the ore form of [Lead](../items/lead_ingot) and when mined drops [Raw Galena](../items/raw_galena). This anti-radiation ingredient is primarily used in recipes for blocks, electronics, and materials.

### Generation
Galena can be found evenly throughout the world between Y 9-96 however it is more concentrated in swamps the lower down you go.

### Smelting
Bauxite ore can be cooked in a furnace or quicker in a blast furnace.
