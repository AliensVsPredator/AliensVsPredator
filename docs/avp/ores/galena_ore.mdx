### Features/Generation

Galena Ore generates in the Overworld, with larger sizes in Swamps. Default values are as follows:

#### Normal
- **Feature ID**: `avp:galena_ore`
- **Count**: 36
- **Height Range**: Uniform (9 to 96)

#### Swamps
- **Feature ID**: `avp:lead_ore_swamp`
- **Count**: 40
- **Height Range**: Trapezoid (-64 to 64)
