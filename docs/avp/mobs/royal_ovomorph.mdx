## Spawning
The royal ovomorph has three primary ways of spawning depending on the settings in the configuration file.
   - Natural spawning
   - World Generation
   - Queen Generation

#### Natural Spawning:
When natural spawning is turned on in the configuration royal_ovomorphs will spawn on resin blocks inside of a hives radius as long as the hive is alive at a very low chance. This setting is on by default.

#### World Generation:
There are specific structures built in the world by a mysterious force that have royal ovomorphs on them.

#### Queen Generation
When a [Queen](./queen) spawns an eggsack she will being to produce [Ovomorphs](./ovomorph) in a 20 block radius around her. There is a very rare chance one of these will be a royal ovomorph. These will carry her genetic information as well.

### Behavior
Royal Ovomorphs will remain still staying secured to the ground with resin. While secure they cannot be pushed even by water. If a player right clicks on a royal ovomorph with sheers it will release the resin holding it, allowing it to be moved but be careful. When a viable host bumps, touches, or gets withing 2 blocks of a royal ovomorph it will trigger it to open, when this occurs a [Royal Facehugger](./mobs/royal_facehugger) is spawned and will attempt to attach to the closest host it can find. When damaged a royal ovomorph will bleed acid.

### Variants
Due to the xenomophs adaptive nature there isn't just one type of royal ovomorph or caste of the creatures. Below is the various types of royal ovomorphs, their differences, and how to obtain them.

#### Royal Nether Ovomorph

  width="20%"
  title="Royal Nether Ovomorph"
  location="avp:royal_nether_ovomorph_wiki"
/>
This type of ovomorph is found in Nether Wastes and in Crimson Forests. With natural spawning turned on the eggs will appear throughout the biomes, otherwise you will need to find them by locating a queen. The coloring of the ovomorph adapted to resemble gilded blackstone in order to attract their most common host piglins. When damaged they also bleed acid however their acid is blue and catches fire when exposed to the air.

#### Royal Aberrant Ovomorph
<ModAsset
  width="20%"
  title="Royal Aberrant Ovomorph"
  location="avp:royal_aberrant_ovomorph_wiki"
/>
This type of ovomorph can only be obtained through special means. You will need to apply [Poison Jelly](../items/poison_jelly) to a royal ovomorph. Once applied the egg will become corrupted forever changing it into a royal aberrant ovomorph, assuming it then finds a host it will become an [Aberrant Queen](./queen) and then through her you can obtain this rare ovomorph. All things considered the xenomorphs from these eggs are weaker than the standard strain but present certain benefits to an intelligent player.

### Drops

Royal Ovomorphs will drop [Resin Balls](../items/resin_ball) when right clicked with sheers.
