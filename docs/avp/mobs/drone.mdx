## Spawning
The ovomorph has three primary ways of spawning depending on the settings in the configuration file.
   - Natural spawning
   - World Generation
   - Queen Generation

#### Natural Spawning:
When natural spawning is turned on in the configuration ovomorphs will spawn on resin blocks inside of a hives radius as long as the hive is alive. This setting is on by default.

#### World Generation:
There are several structures in the world that have ovomorphs inside.

#### Queen Generation
When a [Queen](./queen) spawns an eggsack she will being to produce ovomorphs in a 20 block radius around her. These will carry her genetic information as well.

### Behavior
Ovomorphs will remain still staying secured to the ground with resin. While secure they cannot be pushed even by water. If a player right clicks on a ovomorph with sheers it will release the resin holding it, allowing it to be moved but be careful. When a viable host bumps or touches an ovomorph it will trigger it to open, when this occurs a facehugger is spawned and will attempt to attach to the closest host it can find. When damaged an ovomorph will bleed acid.

### Variants
Due to the [Xenomorphs](./xenomorph) adaptive nature there isn't just one type of ovomorph or caste of the creatures. Below is the various types of ovomorphs, their differences, and how to obtain them.

#### Nether Ovomorph

  title="Nether Ovomorph"
  location="avp:ovomorph_nether_wiki"
/>
This type of ovomorph is found in Nether Wastes and in Crimson Forests. With natural spawning turned on the eggs will appear throughout the biomes, otherwise you will need to find them by locating a queen. The coloring of the ovomorph adapted to resemble gilded blackstone in order to attract their most common host piglins. When damaged they also bleed acid however their acid is blue and catches fire when exposed to the air.

#### Aberrant Ovomorph
<ModAsset
  width="20%"
  title="Aberrant Ovomorph"
  location="avp:ovomorph_aberrant_wiki"
/>
This type of ovomorph can only be obtained through special means. You will need to apply [Poison Jelly](./items/poison_jelly) to a [Royal Ovomorph](./royal_ovomorph). Once applied the egg will become corrupted forever changing it into a [Aberrant Royal Ovomorph](./royal_ovomorph), assuming it then finds a host it will become a queen and then through her you can obtain this rare ovomorph. All things considered the xenomorphs from these eggs are weaker than the standard strain but present certain benefits to an intelligent player.

### Drops

Ovomorphs will drop [Resin Balls](.../items/resin_ball) when right clicked with sheers.
