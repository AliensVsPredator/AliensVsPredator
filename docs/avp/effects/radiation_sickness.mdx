## Description
when exposed to enviromental radiation or picking up an irradiated item you get the radiation sickness I(duration 4 mins) after holding the item longer than 4 minutes the sickness will rise to radiation sickness II (duration 8mins) after holding the item longer than 8 minutes you get radiation sickenss III (duration 16)
